Historic Mechelen · Contemporary comfort

A small hotel with a very special point of view.

The 33 rooms of Hotel 3 Paardekens combine the character of a historic property with the essentials of a comfortable city stay. Its defining feature is upstairs: a glass-enclosed breakfast room with a striking view of St Rumbold's Cathedral.
